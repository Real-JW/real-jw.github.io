About Me

Hi there! Welcome to Jiawen's homepage.

I am a Ph.D. Student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Jeyavijayan (JV) Rajendran. I began my doctoral studies in Fall 2023.

My research interests are focused on hardware security, computer architecture, zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP), hardware accelerators, LLM agents for electronic design automation (EDA), and their intersections.

I serve as a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems (TCAD), IEEE PES Transactions on Smart Grid.
